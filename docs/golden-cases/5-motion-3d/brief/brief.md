# Golden Case 5: Motion/3D — AeroDrone Product Launch

## Brief

**Project**: AeroDrone X1 — Consumer Drone Launch Campaign
**Domain**: Consumer Electronics / Motion Design
**Budget**: $25,000–$35,000
**Timeline**: 5 weeks

### Background
AeroDrone is launching their flagship X1 folding drone (4K camera, 30min flight, GPS follow-me). The launch video must drive 100K pre-orders and establish AeroDJI-killer positioning in a market dominated by DJI.

### Target Audience
- Primary: Tech enthusiasts 25–40, early adopters
- Secondary: Content creators, vloggers
- Tertiary: Gift buyers (holiday season)
- Demographics: Urban, HHI $60K+, YouTube-native

### Deliverables
1. Hero launch video (60s, 4K)
2. Social cutdowns (15s, 30s, 60s)
3. Product reveal animation (30s)
4. 3D product model (folded + unfolded)
5. 3D environment (urban + nature)
6. Motion graphics package (lower thirds, transitions)
7. Sound design + music licensing
8. Endcard + CTA animation
9. Vertical versions (9:16 for Stories/Reels/TikTok)
10. Behind-the-scenes (2 min)

### Constraints
- Must render in 4K (3840x2160) minimum
- Must include vertical (9:16) versions
- Must be licensable for 3 years global
- Must avoid DJI trademark similarities
- Must include closed captions
- Must support HDR delivery

### Success Metrics
- Video completion rate: >70% (60s)
- Pre-order conversion: >2% from video landing page
- Social shares: >50K organic
- Brand recall: >45% aided recall
- Cost per view: <$0.03
