# Golden Case 3: E-commerce — Bloom Skincare

## Brief

**Project**: Bloom — D2C Skincare Product Launch
**Domain**: D2C E-commerce / Beauty
**Budget**: $12,000–$18,000
**Timeline**: 4 weeks

### Background
Bloom is a clean-beauty startup launching their hero product (vitamin C serum) in a crowded market. The product page must convert at 3%+ and support $50K/month ad spend with positive ROAS.

### Target Audience
- Primary: Women 25–40, skincare enthusiasts, ingredient-literate
- Secondary: Gift buyers (partner/friend)
- Demographics: Urban, HHI $75K+, Instagram-native
- Values: Clean ingredients, sustainability, clinical proof

### Deliverables
1. Product page (desktop + mobile)
2. Shopify theme customization
3. Product photography direction
4. Instagram ad creative (5 variations)
5. Facebook/IG story templates
6. Email capture popup
7. Abandonment recovery flow
8. Size/variant selector
9. Ingredient highlight section
10. Review/social proof module

### Constraints
- Must load <3s on mobile 4G
- Must match existing brand guidelines (provided)
- Must support Shopify 2.0 sections
- Must be accessible (screen reader friendly)
- Must support USD, EUR, GBP currencies

### Success Metrics
- Conversion rate: >3.5% (baseline: 1.8%)
- Bounce rate: <40%
- Add-to-cart rate: >8%
- Page load time: <3s (mobile)
- Email capture rate: >5%
