# Golden Case 4: Print — Meridian Annual Report

## Brief

**Project**: Meridian Capital — FY2026 Annual Report
**Domain**: Financial Services / Investor Relations
**Budget**: $20,000–$30,000
**Timeline**: 6 weeks

### Background
Meridian Capital ($2B AUM) needs a 48-page annual report for investors, regulators, and prospects. The report must convey stability, growth, and transparency while meeting SEC filing requirements.

### Target Audience
- Primary: Institutional investors, pension funds
- Secondary: SEC, FINRA regulators
- Tertiary: High-net-worth prospects
- Demographics: 45–65, financially literate, risk-aware

### Deliverables
1. Cover design (print + digital)
2. Interior layout (48 pages)
3. Financial data visualization (12 charts)
4. Infographic spreads (4 pages)
5. CEO letter template
6. Portfolio company spotlight (8 pages)
7. ESG section (6 pages)
8. Print-ready PDF (CMYK, bleed)
9. Digital interactive PDF
10. Accessibility-tagged PDF

### Constraints
- Must comply with SEC EDGAR filing requirements
- Must support print (CMYK, 300dpi) and digital (RGB)
- Must include bleed (3mm) and safe zones
- Must be accessible (PDF/UA, tagged)
- Must support English + Chinese (bilingual edition)

### Success Metrics
- Print quality: Zero defects in 5,000 copy run
- Accessibility: PDF/UA compliant
- Investor feedback: >4.5/5 satisfaction
- Regulatory: Zero SEC comments
- Digital engagement: >3 min average time on page
