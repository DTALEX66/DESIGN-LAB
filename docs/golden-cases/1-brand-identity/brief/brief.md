# Golden Case 1: Brand Identity — NexuTech

## Brief

**Project**: NexuTech — Enterprise AI Platform Brand Identity
**Domain**: B2B SaaS / Artificial Intelligence
**Budget**: $15,000–$25,000
**Timeline**: 6 weeks

### Background
NexuTech is a Series B startup providing AI-powered analytics for enterprise procurement. They need a complete brand identity system that signals trust, intelligence, and approachability.

### Target Audience
- Primary: CPOs, VP Procurement at Fortune 500
- Secondary: Procurement managers, IT decision-makers
- Demographics: 35–55, data-driven, skeptical of "AI-washing"

### Brand Attributes
1. **Authoritative** — proven, reliable, enterprise-grade
2. **Intelligent** — smart, insightful, data-native
3. **Human** — approachable, not cold or robotic
4. **Modern** — contemporary, not dated tech aesthetics

### Deliverables
1. Primary logo + wordmark
2. Logo variations (horizontal, vertical, icon-only, monochrome)
3. Color palette (primary, secondary, neutrals, semantic)
4. Typography system (display, body, mono)
5. Iconography set (24 icons)
6. Photography style guide
7. Brand guidelines document (PDF, min 20 pages)
8. Figma component library
9. Business card design
10. Letterhead + envelope template

### Constraints
- Must work in dark and light modes
- Must be accessible (WCAG AA contrast)
- Must avoid blue/purple gradients (overused in AI)
- Must be reproducible in print and digital
- Must include Chinese character compatibility analysis

### Success Metrics
- Brand recall test: >60% unaided recall after 1 week
- Stakeholder approval: unanimous C-suite sign-off
- Design system adoption: >80% team adoption in 3 months

## Reject Criteria

1. Logo resembles existing tech logos (negative search required)
2. Color palette fails WCAG AA contrast check
3. Typography not available for commercial use
4. Guidelines document <20 pages
5. Missing dark mode variant
