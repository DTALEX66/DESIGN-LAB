The dedicated workbench-browser-e2e job now produces download-proof E2
evidence instead of a silent no-artifact green:

- browser_design_layer_e2e.mjs writes a machine-readable
  browser-e2e-summary.json on EVERY exit path (PASS and FAIL, plus the
  config-missing / token-shape paths), stamping subjectSha, workflowRunId and

  before teardown.

  failure, the honest answer is the skip reason.

  E2E_SUBJECT_SHA / E2E_WORKFLOW_RUN_ID, and a new upload-artifact step

  actions/upload-artifact@65c4c4a1ddee5b7f698f8297e8f3f4f7e4b8c1a2) ships the
  evidence as workbench-e2-<sha>. A clean runner that skips can no longer
  upload an empty artifact and pass.
