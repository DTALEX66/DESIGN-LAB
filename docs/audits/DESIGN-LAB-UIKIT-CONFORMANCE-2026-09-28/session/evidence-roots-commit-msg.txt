WHY

src/design_lab/governance/reporting.py:_safe gates every path an evidence
receipt or projection input may cite. Its `allowed` set held 8 of the 13
repository roots AUTHORITY.md section 7 declares ("仓库责任"). Consequences:

    the front end AUTHORITY section 3 declares first-class ("DESIGN-LAB 有前端：

    front-end fact. That is a governance gap, not a safety property.
  * An audit of the set against section 7 found `research` and `vendor` missing
    too -- the identical defect class. Leaving only `apps` fixed would have
    re-created the gap for section 7's own declared roots.

This was found by the project survey and recorded as finding F-6. The previous
PR (174) deliberately did NOT relax the policy unilaterally; it recorded the gap
and left the decision to the owner. This PR carries the authorization.
