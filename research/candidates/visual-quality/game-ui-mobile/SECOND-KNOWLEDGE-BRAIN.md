## 1. Core Concepts & Frameworks

### 1.1 Mobile Game UI/UX & Touch Interaction Design — Foundational Methods

### 1.1 Thumb zones
Natural, comfortable, stretch zones; one-handed reach; place primary actions in natural zone; Fitts’s law (closer/larger = faster).
### 1.2 Targets
>=44-48 px targets (Apple HIG 44, Material 48), spacing to avoid mis-taps, active area vs visual.
### 1.3 Gestures/readability
Gestures vs buttons, discoverability (affordances, hints); density, contrast (WCAG), font size, sun readability.
### 1.4 Responsive/accessibility
Orientation, dynamic type, one-handed mode, reachability, assistive tech (VoiceOver/TalkBack), colorblind.

Knowledge categories covered:
